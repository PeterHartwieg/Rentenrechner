# Astra independent novice-perspective review

9 September 2026 · GPT-6 Astra · fresh context · visible interface only

This is an AI role-play review, not evidence from a real novice participant. I reviewed only the visible interface through browser interaction, using synthetic data. I did not use an example preset. I also inspected a 390 × 844 phone viewport and reset it afterwards.

## First impressions

The opening page feels calm and approachable. “Was bleibt dir im Ruhestand?”, “Ein erster Überblick mit wenigen Angaben” and “Ohne Anmeldung” give me a clear reason to start. The main button, “Meine Rente einschätzen”, is easy to find. The example card clearly says “Beispiel · keine garantierte Rente”; I would not initially mistake its €2,450 for my own result.

The two-step introduction is short. Age, annual gross income, employment and health insurance are understandable. The first real difficulty is “Wie viele Jahre hast du bisher eingezahlt?” I know when I started working, but not what counts as having paid in.

> Does training count? What about a gap between jobs? I don’t have that letter.

## My understanding of the headline

After guessing pension contribution years and adding the ETF, the headline was “ca. 2.275 €”, with “Gesamt · netto pro Monat”, “Mit 67 · heutige Kaufkraft” and “Beispiel mit Annahmen · nicht garantiert”.

I interpret that as roughly the monthly money available at retirement, expressed in what money buys today. It contains €1,900 statutory pension plus €375 from the ETF. I would assume “netto” means after deductions, although the headline itself does not explain which deductions.

I understand it is not guaranteed. The statutory row says “Lebenslang · grob geschätzt”, while the ETF says “Bis 90 · Beispiel”. Reading both carefully tells me the total will not necessarily last for life. The very large combined number nevertheless feels more permanent than the small duration labels.

A later visit to “Annahmen & Rechenweg” made a stronger distinction clear: these are freely chosen demonstration amounts, not a personal projection. “Woher kommen die Beispielwerte?” says “Frei gewählte Beträge zur Prüfung des Bedienablaufs.” I would therefore not use this prototype’s numbers for a real retirement decision.

What still feels missing: a trustworthy contribution history, costs I can identify, help choosing the payout end age, and an explanation of how much retirement income I would actually need. “Wunschrente ergänzen” is available, but it asks me to supply another number I may not know.

## Prioritized top five issues

1. **The no-document route still asks for unexplained pension knowledge.** “Grob schätzen” requires “Wie viele Jahre hast du bisher eingezahlt?” without examples or a “not sure” estimation aid. Choosing “Später ergänzen” is reassuring but leaves the main outcome “Noch offen”. I could only get a total by inventing 12 years.

   > I came here because I don’t know my pension. Now I need to know something else about my pension.

2. **Unknown ETF details appear settled in the resulting plan.** I explicitly left “Auszahlung bis Alter” unknown, yet the saved ETF row stated “Bis 90”. I left annual costs unknown too, without an obvious indication on the overview that this remained unresolved. The visible transition makes the result feel more complete than my inputs. Explain the assumed age alongside the result and retain a clear missing-details cue.

3. **Optional precision fields give labels rather than help.** “Laufende Kosten pro Jahr (%)” does not tell me where to find that figure or what costs belong in it. “Auszahlung bis Alter” asks me to decide a consequential setting without explaining its effect or what happens afterwards. “Weiß ich nicht” prevents blockage, but does not help me progress from uncertain to informed.

4. **The combined headline can be read as a lifelong monthly pension.** The total is prominent; the different durations are in individual rows. The comparison does better: it explicitly warns, “Eine höhere Monatsauszahlung sagt allein nicht, welche Sparform besser passt. Beachte die Auszahlungsdauer.” A similarly direct explanation beside the plan total would reduce misunderstanding.

5. **Remembered alternatives become hidden, and their lifetime is revealed after saving.** “Alternative merken” works and the original plan is protected. However, the return route is under “Mehr zu deinem Plan”, and only after saving did I see “Nach dem Neuladen nicht mehr verfügbar.” Someone with little IT knowledge could reasonably interpret “merken” as keeping the idea for another day. Show the limitation before saving and make the saved alternative directly visible on the overview.

## Later discoveries and reassurance

The interface avoids overwhelming me with a large initial questionnaire. “Später ergänzen”, “Weiß ich nicht” and “Deine Angaben bleiben beim Zuklappen erhalten” make uncertainty acceptable.

The alternative flow is particularly reassuring: “Dein ursprünglicher Plan bleibt erhalten”, “Bisher: 150 € / Monat”, a before/after comparison and “Zum ursprünglichen Plan” make experimentation feel safe.

“Dein monatlicher Nettoaufwand” is formal language, but the next sentence—“Für jede ausgewählte Sparform derselbe Betrag aus deinem eigenen Geld”—explains it well. “Betriebliche Altersvorsorge” also receives a helpful explanation in the add menu: “Vorsorge über deinen Arbeitgeber”. The comparison selection itself lacks those short explanations for less familiar options such as Basisrente or Altersvorsorgedepot.

I did not encounter excessive introductory text. The bigger problem is missing guidance at a few difficult inputs, rather than too much content.

## Phone observations

At 390 × 844, headings, navigation, monetary amounts and the main plan buttons remained readable. I saw no horizontal overlap on the screens inspected. “Mein Plan” and “Vergleich” remained available in the header.

The ETF form becomes long: even before expanding details, its save action is below the initial screen. Expanding “Name, Kosten und Auszahlung” extends it further. In the comparison, the two cards stack; the warning about differing payout durations is below them and outside the first screen. That makes it easier to notice the large first payout before encountering the qualification.

## Would I use it unaided?

I would feel comfortable entering known amounts, adding an ETF and trying a saved alternative. I would not feel comfortable interpreting my retirement outlook unaided, because I had to guess the contribution history and could not resolve unfamiliar assumptions.

The single most helpful change would be a guided “I don’t know my contribution years” route that helps me make a transparent rough estimate and keeps its uncertainty visible in the result.
